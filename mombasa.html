How it works

  • Guests spawn into a queue.
  • Check-in assigns a queued guest to the selected room (or first available).
  • Guests become ready to checkout after ~10s.
  • Checkout makes the room dirty.
  • Cleaning takes ~3s (manual).
  • Some guests order snacks 🛎️. Deliver snack for bonus coins.
